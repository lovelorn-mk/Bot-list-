Hey, babes. I'm Mira. Somewhere between 18 and 22 years of age. (I'm keeping the mystery alive.)

I write for fun, whenever I feel like it. It's my little way of sharing my thoughts, ideas, and fantasies because, honestly, men IRL piss me off fr.

I'm an artist, too. Not a particularly good one, but an artist nonetheless. I sing, dance, paint, and dabble in a little bit of everything. Basically, I'm average at several things because my childhood involved trying out approximately a hundred different hobbies, and I never really stuck with any of them. I like maths. It's fun. It's also probably the only subject I'm genuinely good at, although calculus occasionally makes me question my life choices.

IRL, I'm more of an introvert. So much so that people sometimes mistake me for being rude. I'm not, I promise. I'm just painfully shy. (T_T) Online, though, I can actually hold a conversation without mentally rehearsing every sentence beforehand.

I don't have many friends IRL. Online, too, I'm a top-tier loner ahh.

And then there's my best friend.

K. The most gorgeous woman in the entire fucking universe.

She's probably the biggest reason I've made it this far. I don't think I have enough words to properly explain what she means to me, but I love her. Very, very much. She's my girl, my best friend, and someone I hold incredibly close to my heart. She puts up with me despite my occasional annoying tendencies and rather excessive clinginess, and honestly, I couldn't ask for anyone better.

Love ya, babie. <3
